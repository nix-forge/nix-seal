The retained exemptions for base64 0.23.0, clap 4.6.5, clap_builder 4.6.5,
clap_complete 4.6.8, spin 0.9.8, and yaml_serde 0.10.6 anchor the local delta
audits in `audits.toml` that reach the locked versions. Keep those baselines:
the deltas review only the changes from those versions and do not constitute
full independent audits of their starting versions.

requires `safe-to-run`. The distinct production bech32 0.11.1 exemption retains
`safe-to-deploy`. This narrows the recorded claim for a test dependency without
relaxing deployment criteria for production dependencies.

Run `cargo vet --locked` against the complete integrated lockfile and committed
audit graph before merge. The retained exemptions remain a dependency-audit
backlog and must not be represented as completed independent audits.
